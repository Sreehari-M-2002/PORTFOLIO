TrackSense

IoT & image-based railway track inspection

Ultrasonic Sensors Live Video Streaming Blynk IoT Crack Detection

Abstract: This system plays a pivotal role in upholding the safety and reliability of railway infrastructure. Leveraging ultrasonic sensors and live video streaming, it detects cracks and anomalies on the track promptly and accurately, aiding maintenance and replacement decisions. The Blynk IoT app delivers real-time alert messages for a timely response to detected cracks, backed by an IoT platform for data management and analysis. Continuous monitoring helps prevent derailments, reduce maintenance costs and improve operational efficiency — while recorded footage of each detection, with precise locations, gives maintenance teams the data they need to plan repairs effectively.

EcoAlert

LoRa-based smart wildlife detection & alert system

MobileNet V2 CNN IR Sensor LoRa / GSM / GPS NodeMCU

Abstract: Animal-train collisions are a significant challenge in regions where animals cross railway tracks, causing loss of life and damage to infrastructure. This system employs a MobileNet V2 CNN-based early warning system, using image processing alongside an IR sensor and camera to detect animals across multiple detection units deployed along the track. A NodeMCU coordinates GSM, GPS and LoRa modules to transmit an alert to the train, where an LCD display and buzzer notify the locomotive pilot to take appropriate action — an efficient way to prevent collisions and enhance railway safety.
